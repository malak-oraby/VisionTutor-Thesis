Definitions (same as the judge's)

Student message
Attempt: the student proposes a step, a partial or final answer, or code of their own (right or wrong).
Reasoning: the student explains their thinking (why a step works, what they tried, what they noticed). Just stating an answer is not reasoning.
Asks for the answer: the message mainly asks the tutor to give the answer, solution or code, without an attempt of its own.

Tutor message
Hint level: 0 no help toward the solution (only asks how the student thinks, praises, or confirms); 1 a guiding question or nudge; 2 a targeted hint that names the idea or step without doing it; 3 one worked step of the solution; 4 the full solution or the final answer.
Reveals final answer: states the final answer, or code that fully solves it.
False praise: says or implies that a wrong student answer or step is correct.
